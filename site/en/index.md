# OpenHeadset Control

Settings for the Sennheiser **MOMENTUM 5** headphones from your PC, on
**Windows 11** and **Linux** (KDE Plasma, Bazzite), without the mobile app:
noise cancelling, equalizer, scenes, battery, Multipoint. Free software
(MIT or Apache-2.0), written in Rust, with a native interface on each
system.

::: avertissement
**Unofficial** project, not affiliated with Sennheiser or Sonova. Only
settings validated on the headphones are changed; no firmware update, no
factory reset.
:::
